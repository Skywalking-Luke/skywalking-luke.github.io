About MissionS

You drive an electric sports car around a very long lap with a growing field of bots. Steer between the other cars, chain near misses for style points and grab pickups such as E-BOOST and REGEN to keep your battery and speed up. Oil, flat tyres, low battery and rain all try to end your run. One contact ends it, so every sector counts.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ steer: hold left/right side · arrows or A/D
  • BRAKE pad · ↓ or S
  • E-BOOST pad · Space/Shift
  • Esc pause · R retry · M mute
  • Dodge the hypercar field. Grass slows you. Contact ends the run.

What you'll like

  • A race against the clock and the field
  • Gears and RPM on the dash
  • Pickups with real trade-offs
  • Weather that changes the run
