About Pace Notes

Pace Notes is a pixel rally game against the clock. Your co-driver calls the corners ahead, such as "RIGHT 5 LONG", and warns you about cliffs. Use the handbrake to drift, hit jumps and pick up checkpoints for extra time. Trees and cliffs end the run, so learn what each call means.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ hold left/right · arrows or A/D
  • BRAKE ↓/S · HANDBRAKE pad / Space (tight turns, drifts)
  • Notes: number = corner speed (1 hairpin … 6 flat)
  • Esc pause · R retry · M mute (also mutes the co-driver)

What you'll like

  • Calls you learn to read
  • Drifts that feel good
  • A stage you can improve a little each run

Questions

What do the numbers mean?
They show how fast you can take the corner: 1 is a hairpin, 6 is flat out.
Do I need an account?
No.
