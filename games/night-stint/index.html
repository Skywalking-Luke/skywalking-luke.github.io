About Night Stint

In Night Stint you drive a long stint in a field of cars as the light fades and your headlights come on. Flash your lights to ask slower cars to move over. When the blue flag shows, let the faster cars through. Damage builds up, slow zones cut your speed and PUSH gives a burst of pace once you have earned it. Beat the stint's par time to move on to the next driver.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ hold left/right · arrows or A/D
  • BRAKE ↓/S · FLASH lights F/W (slower car ahead moves over) · PUSH Space (charged by points)
  • Yellow SLOW ZONES: stay under 80 km/h · Esc pause · R retry · M mute

What you'll like

  • A day-to-night drive
  • A game about patience as much as speed
  • A clear goal for each stint

Questions

Why yield to some cars?
Faster cars catch you from behind. Letting them by keeps your stint clean.
Do I need an account?
No.
