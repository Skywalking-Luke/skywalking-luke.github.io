About Turbo GP

Turbo GP is a pixel open-wheel race on the fictional LH Ring. Choose your tyres on the grid, wait for the lights to go out and race. Open DRS in the green zones when you are within a second of the car ahead, deploy ERS for extra speed and pit when your tyres fade. If rain arrives, you have to decide whether to gamble. Finish inside the points cut to carry on.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ hold left/right · arrows or A/D
  • BRAKE ↓/S · DRS pad / W (in green zones when within 1 s) · ERS hold / Space
  • PIT: steer right onto pit lane on the PIT STRAIGHT · 1-4 pick tyres
  • Esc pause · R retry · M mute

What you'll like

  • Simple controls with real strategy
  • Short races
  • Rain that changes the plan

Questions

What do DRS and ERS do?
DRS opens in green zones to help you pass. ERS is a stored boost you deploy yourself.
Do I need an account?
No.
