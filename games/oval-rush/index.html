About Oval Rush

Oval Rush puts you in a pack of cars on a big oval. Tuck in behind the car ahead to draft, then pull out and slingshot past. Fuel and tyres wear down, so dive left onto pit road at the right time. Now and then a multi-car wreck, the Big One, sweeps the field. Make the cut line at the end of each stage to stay in the race.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ hold left/right · arrows or A/D
  • BRAKE pad · ↓/S · Esc pause · R retry · M mute
  • Sit 1 car behind to DRAFT, pull out to SLINGSHOT.
  • PIT: dive left onto pit road (grey lane) on the frontstretch.

What you'll like

  • Drafting you can feel
  • A pit-road decision every stage
  • A pack that never stays the same

Questions

How do I get past someone?
Draft behind them, then steer out for the slingshot.

No.
