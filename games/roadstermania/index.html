About Roadstermania

You drive a white roadster through traffic, tunnels, toll gates and traffic lights. Tap to roll over the cars in your way. Hold to climb, and in the tunnels keep holding to stick to the ceiling and drive upside down while the jam crawls past below. Let go and you drop back to the road. Mistime the landing and it is a crash, so expect to press RETRY a lot.

Every roll over a car scores. Chain rolls before you land to build combos, and fill the BOOST meter for a burst of speed through the tight sections. Reach the landing arch to clear a level, unlock the next one, then go back and beat your own score.

How to play

  • TAP roll over cars
  • HOLD climb or stick to the ceiling
  • LET GO fall back to the road
  • Every 5000 pts = 1 BOOST (max 3). BOOST button, Shift or B.
  • Roll over cars for +100. Chain rolls before landing for up to x5.

What you'll like

  • Short runs and instant retry
  • A one-button idea you learn in seconds
  • Combos that reward nerve
  • Plays on phone and desktop
