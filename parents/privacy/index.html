Little Racers privacy

Short and plain: we collect nothing.

  • No ads. Little Racers never shows adverts and loads no ad code.
  • No cookies and no analytics. There is no tracking and no visit counter on Little Racers.
  • No accounts. Nobody signs in and we never ask for a name or email.
  • Nothing sent about your child. Progress and settings are kept only on your own device, in your browser, and you can clear them any time by clearing the site data.
  • No outside services. The games do not load scripts, fonts or media from other websites.
