Little Racers

Calm car games for little ones aged 2-5.

Open Little Racers

Little Racers is a set of gentle car games for toddlers. Honk a horn, wash a car, build one and wander around a cosy Malaysian kampung. It works in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

For parents

  • No ads.
  • No trackers and no data collected.
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • A rest screen after about 15 minutes of play, which a grown-up clears by typing YES.
  • Easy to stop at any time. Progress stays on your own device.

Good to know

What age is it for?
Ages 2 to 5. The games get a little harder as children get older.
What languages does it use?
English and Bahasa Malaysia.
Does it need an internet connection or an account?
It opens in your browser and needs no account or sign-in.
What do you collect about my child?
Nothing. There are no ads, no cookies and no analytics on Little Racers. Read the Little Racers privacy note.
