NO ACCOUNTS

You never sign in to play. We do not ask for your name, email or any account details.

LOCAL STORAGE

Roadstermania and Mission S save your leaderboard (3-letter initials, scores, times), progress and sound settings in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clear your browser's site data to delete it.

VISIT COUNTER

To see which arcade games people enjoy, we count visits and plays for each game as daily totals. There are no cookies, no accounts and no personal IDs, and we do not store your IP address. Your browser keeps one small value, the day number of your last visit to a game, so we can tell whether a visit is a return and roughly how long ago. Nothing is sent if your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control switched on. The counter runs on Cloudflare, which handles your connection like any web host. It is used only on the arcade pages, never on Little Racers.
