About LH Cars

Small racing games, made in Malaysia.

LH Cars makes small, free racing games for the browser. Each one is a short, easy-to-learn take on a different kind of racing: a one-button night run, a long lap against a field of bots, pack racing on an oval, a grand prix, endurance driving and rally. The cars, tracks and names are our own.

The games are free to play, with no account and no sign-in. Your scores stay on your own device. The site pays for itself through adverts on our game and information pages. Little Racers, the calm car games for ages 2-5, has no ads and no tracking at all.

Have feedback, a bug to report or an idea for a new game? See the contact page.
